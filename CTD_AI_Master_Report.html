The GEN AI Program, by the Numbers

A full read-only breakdown of the CTD generative-AI pipeline: how much AI is in the cuts, how efficiently we're generating it, and where every shot stands. Sourced entirely from the CTD Airtable — GEN AI SHOTCODES, GEN AI ASSETS and GEN AI REPORTS, plus CUTS TO NETWORK and the 08.12 day-to-day schedule. Snapshot August 14, 2026.

The one-sentence version: generative AI now fills between 29.7% and 41.1% of every measured cut — a weighted 35.9% of program runtime — we're producing it roughly three times more efficiently than when we started, and 56% of all planned shots are already cut-ready and delivered.

AI is a structural third-plus of the show. Across four independent cuts on four different episodes the share sits between 29.7% and 41.1%, and it does not shrink as a cut matures — 102 climbed from 32.5% at rough cut to 41.1% at picture lock, and 103 went from 27.9% at rough cut to 33.3% at fine cut. Every episode has gained AI on the way to lock, not lost it.

02Efficiency — are we improving?

Clips generated per clip that lands in the cut. Generated = all clips delivered for that episode's stories; Used = AI placements in that cut's timeline.

Yes — sharply, then it settled. 101 needed 5.61 generations per clip that made the cut. 102, 103 and 104 land at 2.47, 2.53 and 1.75 — roughly a third of 101's waste for more AI on screen. That's the locked prompt library, the reference-frame workflow and re-spin discipline compounding, and the curve has now flattened: ~2:1 looks like the floor with the current toolset.

Two caveats for honesty: (1) 101 has been in production longest and carries the most one-time experimental and re-spun generations — some of its 5.61:1 was tuition later episodes skip. (2) "In cut" counts timeline placements; a clip used twice counts twice, so true distinct-clip efficiency is marginally leaner. The trend is unambiguous either way.

07Method & sources

AI share: sum of AI clip durations per cut (GEN AI REPORTS), excluding rows flagged outdated or duplicate, over the runtime taken from that cut's final Seq-Out timecode at 24 fps.

Efficiency: total delivered files per episode (GEN AI ASSETS, by episode lookup) divided by AI placements in that episode's latest cut (GEN AI REPORTS).

Pipeline: live status counts from GEN AI SHOTCODES. Re-spins: assets whose "Re-spin For" link is populated — every re-generated file, which is a broader and more reliable count than the older note-text method used in the July report.

Calendar & deadlines: the 08.12 day-to-day schedule, cross-checked against SHOOT DATES and CUTS TO NETWORK in the base.

Reconciling the totals: 37 shotcodes and 24 generated clips carry no episode link in Airtable. They are included in every series-wide total on this site (1,597 shotcodes, 5,032 clips) and excluded from every per-episode figure, so the per-episode columns sum to 1,560 and 5,008 respectively. Both figures are shown as an "unassigned" row wherever a table breaks down by episode. Fixing the links in the base makes the two views identical.

Scope: 3,892 clip placements, 5,032 generated files, 1,597 planned shotcodes, 34 network cuts and 21 shoot days read. Runtime and placements-as-clips are floors and are flagged where they appear; both bias the same direction and don't change any conclusion.
