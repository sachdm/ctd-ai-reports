AI Accuracy Notes for the Team

Quick version — what to change before generating, episodes 102–108. Full audit with sources lives in the Accuracy Audit.

Change before generating

SJE · San Juanico

FIXIt wasn't a "hot summer day" — it was November, ~5:30 a.m., still dark. PipeRuptures, GasGhost01 and ResidentHellPOV all need to go night/pre-dawn, fire-lit.

TAD · Tenerife

FIXThe hills behind Los Rodeos are Tenerife's own, not Gran Canaria's. Fix KLMFlightTaxis + PanAmBehindKLM and any gens made from them.

HMB · Halifax

FIXSwap the ships' energy: Mont-Blanc crept slowly (explosives aboard), Imo was the one speeding. Also: "TBT" → TNT, and the two whistle briefs are labeled backwards.

ORA · Ocean Ranger

FIXThe control-room and radio-room shots are slugged DAY — everything from the broken portlight on happened at night in a winter storm. And the seas were 55 ft, not 35.

MVE · Vesuvius

FIXNo lava. 79 AD was ash, pumice and pyroclastic surges — that's what the shots should show. Also spelling: Vesuvius.

SMI · Sago Mine

FIXCause was a lightning strike, not a cable spark; miners were trapped by toxic air, not collapsed tunnels; 14 men escaped on the mantrip, not 11.

TSF · Triangle Shirtwaist

FIXThe fire ladders reached the 6th floor, not the 7th — that's the famous detail, keep it exact.

AA191 · American 191

FIXThe engine on the runway weighs ~8,600 lb, not 4,000 — fix the copy before it hits a script.

BMF · Boston Molasses

FIXNo elevated railcar floated in the molasses — the El track buckled and the train stopped short. Show buckled structure + a floating wagon instead.

KAL007 · Korean Air 007

FIXOne brief says 727 — it's a 747.

MRB · I-35W Bridge

FIXNo portrait smartphone footage in 2007 — the collapse was caught on a fixed security camera. Collapse-day cars must look 2007.

HSA · Hurricane Sandy

FIXSpelling: Belle Harbor, no "u".

Double-check before shooting

SDS · Sampoong

CHECKDon't show an orderly store-wide evacuation — management never ordered one. That's the scandal.

KAR · Krakatoa

CHECKThe final blast happened under ash darkness, not clear sky; the shockwave broke windows, it didn't flatten forests.

OTI · Titan

CHECKTitanic's lights were out by the time the stern rose; and the "snagged in wreck" beat is from an earlier dive, not June 18.

LPE · Loma Prieta

CHECKThe Cypress had two decks — say "lower level," or the AI will invent a third.

HMB · Halifax

CHECKIgnition was sparking steel as the ships pulled apart (not engine sparks); and confirm whether it was really Mackey who got thrown by the blast before depicting it.
